# Major matching

Each major profile has demand vectors, reviewed sources, requirements, related directions, and exploration tasks. V1 matching calculates:

- **Cognitive fit:** weighted overlap between domain scores and the major demand vector.
- **Interest fit:** overlap with the provisional RIASEC profile.
- **Fit:** 55% cognitive fit + 45% interest fit.
- **Readiness:** current academic and activity evidence, bounded 0–100.
- **Feasibility:** `ready_to_explore`, `feasible_with_preparation`, or `needs_preparation`.
- **Confidence:** completion, uncertainty, evidence amount, and profile freshness.

Recommendations must explain why a major appeared, what may be challenging, what evidence is missing, and what to try next. “Bad fit” is never used.
