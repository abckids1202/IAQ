# IAQ Tracker

Tracker is an evidence timeline, not an intelligence leaderboard. It keeps four views:

- Cognitive history with assessment version, quality, and recommended 9–12 month retest timing.
- Interest evolution after meaningful reflections, not weekly practice effects.
- Readiness development from subjects, projects, activities, and skills.
- Major exploration with concrete steps, reflections, and realistic exposure.

Completed projects and reflections are stronger direction evidence than clicks or page views. A student can pause a session and resume it without losing progress.
