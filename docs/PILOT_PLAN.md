# Pilot plan

1. **Cognitive interviews:** 30–50 high-school students; observe interpretation and clarity.
2. **Closed beta:** approximately 200–500 students across grades and schools; balance forms and collect response time, feedback, conditions, and demographics with consent.
3. **Norming study:** a psychometrician should define a 1,500–3,000 student sample across ages 14–18, grades, regions, school types, and relevant language conditions.
4. **Validation:** compare with established assessments, academic indicators, test–retest stability, counselor interpretation, and later outcomes.
5. **Continuous calibration:** introduce experimental items without scoring, monitor drift and fairness, then promote or retire items with review.

Synthetic responses are useful for software edge cases only. They cannot prove validity or fairness.
