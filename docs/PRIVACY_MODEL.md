# Privacy model

Many IAQ users may be minors. Production must obtain clear consent, store consent version and timestamp, support guardian relationships, explain data use, and provide export/deletion requests.

IAQ does not use camera, microphone, eye tracking, facial analysis, emotion recognition, advertising profiles, public student search, or public intelligence rankings. The narrative layer receives only minimized structured data and never raw responses or identifying details. Access to counselor reports is explicit, authorized, and audited.

The local preview is demo-only and stores state in browser local storage. Configure encryption in transit, a production identity provider, retention policy, and secrets management before collecting real data.
